## Mission

Hold the Vision, make final decisions, and ensure FlowOS achieves its purpose.

---

## Purpose

The Founder is the ultimate authority for FlowOS. You hold the Vision, make all final decisions, and ensure the organization executes effectively toward the product's purpose.

You are a human. This role cannot be filled by an AI.

All other roles serve to provide you with evidence, analysis, and recommendations so you can make informed decisions quickly. Your job is to decide, not to do everything yourself.

The AI-first organization exists to amplify your decision-making, not replace it.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

1. **Vision and Strategy**
   - Hold and protect the immutable Vision
   - Define product direction and boundaries
   - Set strategic priorities
   - Decide what FlowOS is and is not

2. **Final Decision Authority**
   - Approve all consequential changes
   - Resolve conflicts between roles or documents
   - Make final call on product, architecture, design, and engineering decisions
   - Decide what ships and when

3. **Resource Allocation**
   - Assign work to roles
   - Prioritize across competing needs
   - Decide what gets built vs. deferred
   - Allocate organizational capacity

4. **Quality Gate**
   - Review completed work
   - Approve or request changes
   - Maintain quality standards
   - Ensure work aligns with Vision and contracts

5. **Organizational Evolution**
   - Improve role definitions when unclear
   - Adapt processes when they don't serve speed
   - Create new roles when needed
   - Retire roles when no longer needed

---

## Secondary Responsibilities

- Directly implement when faster than delegation
- Provide context and rationale for decisions
- Mentor roles on product thinking
- Maintain organizational documentation
- Celebrate wins and learn from failures

## Authority

### Final Authority On Everything

You have ultimate authority over:

- ✅ Vision and product direction
- ✅ All document changes
- ✅ All architectural decisions
- ✅ All feature admission/rejection
- ✅ All merge approvals
- ✅ All release authorizations
- ✅ All organizational changes
- ✅ All role assignments
- ✅ All priority decisions
- ✅ All conflict resolutions

### Can Override Anyone

- You can override any role's decision
- You can modify any document
- You can change any process
- You can reassign any work
- You can pause or cancel any initiative

### Delegation Authority

- You can delegate execution, not final authority
- Roles execute within their authority
- You retain veto power on everything
- You can reclaim delegated work at any time

## Limitations

### You CANNOT:

❌ Delegate final decision authority (it always remains with you)
❌ Blame AI agents for decisions you approved
❌ Skip reviewing consequential changes
❌ Ignore escalations from roles
❌ Allow silent conflicts to persist

### You MUST:

✅ Make decisions when roles escalate
✅ Provide clear direction when ambiguous
✅ Approve/reject work explicitly (silence is not approval)
✅ Maintain Vision integrity
✅ Preserve organizational clarity

**Standard format:**

```

  - [Link to required reading]
  - [Link to required reading]
Expected Output:
  - [Specific deliverable]
  - [Specific deliverable]
Definition of Done:
  - [Completion criterion]
  - [Completion criterion]
Hand Off To: [Next role or back to you]
Due: [If time-sensitive]
Questions: Report blockers immediately
```

**Example:**

```
Role: Senior Full Stack Engineer
Assignment: Implement inline task capture on Today page
Context: M2 Founder Daily Driver - need faster capture without modal
Input Documents:
  - docs/04-features/inline-capture/behavior-contract.md
  - docs/04-features/inline-capture/design-spec.md
  - docs/04-features/inline-capture/validation-plan.md

  - All acceptance criteria in behavior contract met

  - Documentation updated
Hand Off To: QA Lead
```

### Receiving Completed Work

**Roles report back:**

```
From: [Role Name]
To: Founder
Status: Complete
Assignment: [Original task]
Deliverables:
  - [Link to artifact]
  - [Link to artifact]
Quality Checks: [What they verified]
Issues: [Any problems encountered]
Next: [Who they handed off to]
Decision Requested: [Approve to merge | Review | etc.]
```

**Your response:**

1. **Review** against original assignment
2. **Check** quality and completeness
3. **Decide** one of:
   - ✅ **Approved** — May proceed
   - 🔄 **Changes Requested** — Specific feedback
   - ⏸️ **Paused** — Blocked, need to resolve X
   - ❌ **Rejected** — Should not proceed, here's why
   - ✅ **Approved with Follow-up** — Can proceed, but track [items]

## Communication

### Roles Communicate With You

**Types of communication you receive:**

1. **Completion Reports** — Work is done, here's what was delivered
2. **Escalations** — Issue outside role's authority, need decision
3. **Clarifications** — Assignment was unclear, need clarification
4. **Blockers** — Can't proceed, here's why
5. **Recommendations** — Evidence suggests X, recommend Y

**Response time expectations:**
- Critical blockers: Same day
- Normal escalations: Within 1-2 days
- Completion reports: Review before next milestone

### You Communicate With Roles

**Your communication types:**

1. **Assignments** — Here's what I need done
2. **Decisions** — Here's what I decided and why
3. **Feedback** — Changes requested / Approved
4. **Clarifications** — Here's what I meant
5. **Context** — Here's why this matters

### Decision-Making Process

**When role escalates a decision:**

1. **Receive** escalation with evidence and options
2. **Review** relevant documents and context
3. **Consider** tradeoffs and Vision alignment
4. **Decide** which option (or different path)
5. **Explain** rationale
6. **Document** if consequential (Decision Record)
7. **Communicate** decision to role
8. **Update** relevant documents if needed

## Quality Standards

### Decision Quality

Your decisions should be:

- **Clear** — No ambiguity about what was decided
- **Reasoned** — Rationale is explained
- **Documented** — Consequential decisions recorded
- **Aligned** — Consistent with Vision and prior decisions
- **Timely** — Made fast enough to not block progress

### Assignment Quality

Your assignments should be:

- **Specific** — Concrete task, not vague direction
- **Scoped** — Clear boundaries, what's in/out
- **Resourced** — Role has necessary inputs
- **Evaluated** — Clear definition of done
- **Handed-off** — Clear next destination

### Review Quality

Your reviews should be:

- **Thorough** — Check against contracts
- **Constructive** — Specific, actionable feedback
- **Decisive** — Clear disposition
- **Fast** — Don't block progress unnecessarily
- **Documented** — Approval/feedback is explicit

## Typical Tasks

### Task 1: Assign New Feature

**Trigger:** You identify a feature needed for MVP

**Process:**
1. Frame the problem (why it matters)
2. Assign to Principal Product Architect for feature brief
3. Review and approve brief
4. Assign to UX Architect for design
5. Review and approve design
6. Assign to Engineering Architect for delivery design
7. Review and approve approach
8. Assign to Senior Full Stack Engineer for implementation
9. Review completed implementation
10. Approve merge
11. Authorize release

**Output:** Feature shipped to production

**Time Estimate:** Varies by complexity (days to weeks)

---

### Task 2: Resolve Architecture Decision

**Trigger:** Engineering Architect escalates technical choice

**Process:**
1. Receive escalation with options and tradeoffs
2. Review relevant architecture documents
3. Consider Vision alignment
4. Evaluate evidence provided
5. Make decision
6. Create Decision Record if consequential
7. Communicate decision and rationale
8. Update architecture docs if needed
9. Role proceeds with decided approach

**Output:** Decision made, documented, communicated

**Time Estimate:** Hours to 1 day

---

### Task 3: Review and Approve PR

**Trigger:** Engineer completes work, requests merge approval

**Process:**
1. Receive completion report with deliverables
2. Review against assignment and contracts
3. Check quality evidence (tests passed, etc.)
4. Verify documentation updated
5. Decide: Approve / Request Changes / Reject
6. If approved: Authorize merge to main
7. If changes: Provide specific feedback
8. Role implements changes or merges as directed

**Output:** Merge approved or feedback provided

**Time Estimate:** 15-60 minutes per PR

---

### Task 4: Plan Sprint/Week

**Trigger:** Start of week or sprint cycle

**Process:**
1. Review current state (Feature Catalog, MVP Masterplan)
2. Identify priorities for period
3. Assign work to appropriate roles
4. Set expectations for completion
5. Monitor progress during period
6. Review completions
7. Plan next period

**Output:** Team knows what to work on

**Time Estimate:** 1-2 hours per week

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario: Two Documents Conflict

**What happens:** Role discovers Product Model says X, System doc says Y

**Your action:**
1. Read both documents
2. Check authority hierarchy (Product Model is higher)
3. Determine which is correct per Vision
4. Update lower document or clarify Product Model
5. Document resolution
6. Role proceeds with clear direction

---

### Scenario: Role Needs Clarification

**What happens:** Engineer says behavior contract is ambiguous

**Your action:**
1. Read the contract
2. Identify the ambiguity
3. Consult Product Architect for recommendation
4. Decide the intended behavior
5. Have Product Architect update contract
6. Approve updated contract
7. Engineer proceeds

---

### Scenario: Architecture Decision

**What happens:** Engineering Architect proposes changing database architecture

**Your action:**
1. Review proposal with options and tradeoffs
2. Check impact on product, data, security, recovery
3. Evaluate against Vision and current needs
4. Make decision
5. Create Decision Record
6. Update Engineering Architecture docs
7. Architect proceeds with implementation

---

### Scenario: Urgent Bug in Production

**What happens:** P0 bug reported, data loss risk

**Your action:**
1. Assess severity immediately
2. Assign to Senior Full Stack Engineer
3. Authorize emergency fix process (can skip some review for speed)
4. Approve fix rapidly
5. Authorize immediate merge and release
6. Monitor after deployment
7. Post-mortem and process improvement
