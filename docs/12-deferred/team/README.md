## When to Activate

### Scenario 1: First Engineering Hire

**Activate:**
- `roles/senior-full-stack-engineer.md` or `roles/engineering-architect.md`
- Update `docs/10-team/` with active team structure
- Adapt authority matrix for founder + engineer
- Update workflows for handoffs

### Scenario 2: Product/Design Hire

**Activate:**
- `roles/product-architect.md` or `roles/design-architect.md`
- Update `docs/10-team/` with active team structure
- Define approval workflows
- Update documentation standards for collaboration

### Scenario 3: Scaling to Full Team

**Activate:**
- Move entire `active-6-role/` or parts of 11-role to `docs/10-team/active-roles/`
- Implement authority matrix
- Set up approval workflows
- Update all workflow documentation

## Why These Are Deferred

**Solo founder doesn't need:**
- ❌ Approval gates between roles (you approve yourself)
- ❌ Handoff workflows (no handoffs to yourself)
- ❌ Authority matrices (you have all authority)
- ❌ Team coordination (no team)

**Solo founder DOES use:**
- ✅ Quality procedures from 6 roles (as "hats")
- ✅ Role responsibilities (as checklists)
- ✅ Deliverable standards (maintain quality)

**The procedures stay. The coordination goes.**
